## 💡 Seed Project Ideas

### 1. The True Cost of the Commute (Gauteng)
- **Question:** What does a typical commute in Gauteng really cost in rands and hours, and how does that compare with average earnings?
- **Data:** Open data such as Stats SA's National Household Travel Survey and published minibus taxi route and fare data.
- **Approach:** Clean and model the data with Excel/Power Query and Python, then build an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to explore commute cost and time by area, finishing with three key insights and a recommendation.
- **Why it matters:** Minibus taxis serve most Gauteng commuters, and commute costs shape which jobs and opportunities people can realistically reach.

### 2. Gauteng Minibus Taxi Route and Fare Explorer
- **Question:** Which routes are the longest, the most expensive per kilometre, or the least connected?
- **Approach:** Analyse open route and fare data (GTFS format where available) with Python and SQL, and map the results to highlight gaps in the network.
- **Why it matters:** It shows where better transport information could help commuters plan cheaper and safer journeys.
